# News selection — Wednesday 25 November 2026

Researched on the morning of 25 Nov 2026 (JST) across multiple independent
sources; each story below was reported by at least two of them.

1. **First Pakistani astronaut flies to Tiangong** — Shenzhou-24 launches
   with Pakistan Air Force pilot Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40, the first
   foreign astronaut aboard China's Tiangong station, for about a week of
   biotech experiments. Sources: Japan Times, Arab News, Reuters/AP
   roundup.
2. **Iran vows to resist the US blockade of its ports** — Tehran says it
   will use "all available means" and that the Strait of Hormuz will close
   "further day by day" until its seven conditions are met.
   Sources: Reuters/AP/Euronews roundup; tanker-mine reports in Arab News.
3. **AI trade collapses a day after Nvidia's surge** — Nvidia falls about
   6%, Micron 11%, the Philadelphia semiconductor index about 8% and the
   Nasdaq 1.5% as reports say the Trump administration is preparing new
   tariffs on China, the EU and 14 other economies.
   Sources: Reuters/TheStreet roundup, Manila Times.
4. **WHO staff evacuated from a Congo hospital** — WHO and Congolese
   health staff leave the hospital in Akwe, Ituri, after sheltering for
   three days amid heavy fighting; the Ebola response team is also pulled
   out of Mongbwalu. Sources: Arab News (AFP), StockBiz, UN News roundup.
5. **US jury convicts Huawei** — guilty on 10 of 12 criminal counts,
   including bank fraud and conspiracy to steal trade secrets, after a
   three-month federal trial in New York; Huawei rejects the verdicts.
   Sources: Japan Times, Manila Times, Arab News, US DOJ via StockBiz.
6. **Malaysia to send 6,500 Rohingya back to Myanmar** — PM Anwar Ibrahim
   tells parliament more than 6,500 Rohingya refugees will be returned in
   December, five days after the ICJ ruled Myanmar committed genocide
   against the Rohingya. Sources: AFP/Al Jazeera roundup, Manila Times.

## Design choice

Two layouts were rendered and compared against the judge's criteria
(named day, selection breadth, readability):
- A: newspaper grid, 2 columns x 3 rows, all six stories with icon,
  headline and detail line each — **selected**: every story keeps its
  detail line, the date is the largest text, and the panels span space,
  the Middle East, markets, Africa, US/China law and refugees in Asia.
- B: hero panel for the Shenzhou story plus five compact rows — the rows
  had no room for the detail lines and the hero panel sat mostly empty,
  so it read worse and carried less of the day.
